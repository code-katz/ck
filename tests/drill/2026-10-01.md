Will's decision, 2026-10-01: keep Fable. The rewrite and the finalize stay on River's tier; $8 per PRD is the accepted cost, and the remaining levers are turn counts, not models.

Two more findings:

1. The panel inside `/ck:prd` returned yes-if from all three lenses for the third run in a row, so `panelFailedToDisagree` fired again. The question "Is this PRD ready for the author's review, and what would you change before it ships?" invites yes-if. A question that forces a stance is worth trying before changing anything else about the panel.
2. The harness: a detached run dies about three minutes after the session's turn ends, because the container is reclaimed when idle; a background tool command dies at ten minutes. A measurement longer than ten minutes needs a Monitor armed for its duration, which keeps the session awake. Two attempts were lost learning this (the first at the ten-minute limit after draft, validate, panel, and memo had finished for $4.13; the second at the container reclaim after draft and one revision).
