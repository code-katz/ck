## What happened, in order

1. River's draft was 3,604 words before the appendices against the contract's 3,000 cap. The checker failed it on length alone.
2. River revised; 3,087 words, still over by 87. The checker failed it again.
3. River revised a second time. The third checker did not count: it said the body "appears to exceed" the cap and failed it. The workflow moved on to the panel, as designed after two revisions.
4. The panel ran on three models and wrote its memo. Fine.
5. River's rewrite after the panel came to 3,947 words. The rewrite adds the panel's revisions and never takes anything out.
6. The post-rewrite check failed it on length. River trimmed with ten edits and cut about 540 words; 3,406 remained. The trim produced 30k output tokens, more than any other pass, because each edit carried long passages.
7. The second check failed it. The run ended with a valid document in every respect but length, and a result that says `valid: false`.

## What broke

1. **The cap is reactive.** Nothing tells River the length before writing; every length rule fires after the fact, and each firing costs a Fable pass. Three of River's five passes were about length.
2. **The rewrite grows the document.** The post-panel prompt says "keep it under the cap" at the end of a long instruction, after asking for revisions and two appendices. It landed 947 words over.
3. **The trim has no number to hit.** It is told to cut "whole paragraphs of repetition until at least five percent under the cap" without the current count, and it may not count. It cut 540 of the 1,250 words needed.
4. **One validator guessed.** The pre-panel validator's prompt does not tell it to count with `wc`; the third round failed the document on an impression.
5. **The PRD counts the roster** ("six of 21 tiered personas"), against Will's rule from the brief review: describe the roster, never count it. No agent has been told that rule.

Not broken: the run record (`run.json` with the harness run id), the panel on three models, the memo, the review page, and the resume data on disk.

## The fix in 0.1.4

1. Length first and lower: `targetWords` 2,500 beside the 3,000 cap, stated at the top of the draft prompt.
2. The rewrite after the panel may not be longer than the draft it replaces.
3. The trim receives the checker's count, cuts at least count minus nine tenths of the cap, and may count once at the end.
4. The pre-panel validator counts with `wc` before judging length.
5. Every persona agent's preamble carries the roster rule: describe it, never count it.

Re-measure `/ck:prd` on 0.1.4: expected one draft, one check, panel, one rewrite, one check, no trim; about $9.
