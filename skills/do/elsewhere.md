# /ck:do: a task that belongs elsewhere

Say so in one line that names the command, with the author's subject after it when it takes one. That line is the whole reply.

- A pipeline document: `/ck:opportunity <idea>`, `/ck:market-research [focus]`, `/ck:brief <idea>`, `/ck:prd`, `/ck:team`, `/ck:roadmap`, `/ck:architecture`, `/ck:brand-guide`, or `/ck:design <feature>` for a feature's full design round (variants, every screen state, and a design spec). One mockup of one change is a draw step in `/ck:do`, not this.
- A decision between options: `/ck:panel <the question>`.
- Several separate jobs in one sentence, or one job that plainly needs more than three steps: `/ck:plan <the work>`, which stages it for several sessions, or offer the first job the author named as a task they can type.
