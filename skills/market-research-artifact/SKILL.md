# The market research contract (`<project-repo>/docs/market-research.md`)

A deeper market pass than the brief's: several questions researched on the web, cross-checked, and written up with a source per claim.

## Section order

Use these headings verbatim, in this order.

1. `## Summary`: exactly five findings that change a decision, one sentence each.
2. `## Market size and trends`.
3. `## Competitors and substitutes`: table, name | what it does | who it serves | price or model | strength | gap. At least three rows.
4. `## Customers, segments, and channels`.
5. `## Pricing and business models`.
6. `## Constraints`: platform, legal, regulatory.
7. `## Contradictions and unknowns`: claims the researchers disagreed on, with both sources; what could not be found. Present even when empty.
8. `## Implications for positioning`.
9. `## Sources`: numbered; URL, title, date accessed.

## Checklist

1. Every section is present, in order, with its heading verbatim.
2. Every claim in the body carries a source number that exists in Sources, or is marked unverified.
3. The competitors table has at least three rows.
4. Contradictions and unknowns is present even when empty.
5. No source is older than 18 months unless marked as historical.
6. Summary has exactly five findings.
7. The document is under 2,500 words before Sources.
8. No em-dashes in prose.

## Writing

Plain technical English: name the actor, one instruction per sentence, no filler, no loss of precision.
